# K0100 sound effects: sources and licences

All files here were synthesised on this Mac with ffmpeg's built-in generators (anoisesrc, aevalsrc) and filters,
on 31 Oct 2026, for this episode. No recordings or third-party sounds were downloaded or used, so there is no
third-party licence and no attribution is needed; they are the channel's own work and free to reuse.
