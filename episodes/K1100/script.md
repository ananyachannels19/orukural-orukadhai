# குறள் #1100 · வளையல் பிடிக்காதுன்னு சொன்னவ
*The woman who said she hated bangles* · அதிகாரம் 110: குறிப்பறிதல் (Recognition of the Signs) · காமத்துப்பால் · runtime 38 s

> கண்ணொடு கண்இணை நோக்கொக்கின் வாய்ச்சொற்கள்  
> என்ன பயனும் இல.

**பொருள் (our words):** இரண்டு பேரின் கண்களும் ஒன்றையொன்று புரிந்துகொண்டுவிட்டால், அதன் பிறகு வாயால் சொல்லும் வார்த்தைகள் எந்தப் பயனும் தராது.  
**Meaning:** When two pairs of eyes have already understood each other, the words spoken afterwards add nothing.

**Logline:** At a dusk bangle stall Dharani insists she does not like bangles on her birthday while her eyes keep going to one green set; Naveen asks her to say it looking at him, and the two read each other's eyes: she saw his worry, he saw her wish.  
**Why it's original:** A wife's loud 'I don't like bangles' is undone by her eyes, and a husband's quiet worry by his: each reads the other without a word, with the folded notes he saved in his shirt pocket as the proof.

**Lesson:** கண்ணு பேசினா, வார்த்தை வேணாம்.  
**Tag question:** கண்ணால பேசுற ஜோடியை டேக் பண்ணுங்க!  
**Loop:** The last shot repeats the first shot's framing, now with the green bangles on Dharani's wrist, and the closing tag line leads back to her opening bright 'I don't like bangles', so a replay makes you watch her eyes the second time.

## Continuity

- **bangle_stall**: A pavement bangle stall on a temple street in a Tamil Nadu town: a wooden frame with vertical bamboo pegs stacked with glass bangles in red, yellow, blue and green, a faded blue tarpaulin awning above with a string of warm yellow bulbs, a small wooden stool, a temple gopuram in silhouette far behind, other stalls softly blurred. *Light:* Dusk, indigo sky with temple-lamp gold light and warm bulb light on faces; the same in every shot. *Blocking:* Camera in front of the stall; Dharani stands centre-left facing the camera, Naveen at her right shoulder (frame right) turned towards her; the bangle pegs are behind them at the left, with the green set at eye level; nobody walks away.
- prop **bangles** (வளையல்): Exactly one set of a dozen green glass bangles with tiny gold dots, tied with a red thread, hanging on a peg among the other colours until Naveen slides them onto Dharani's wrist.
- prop **notes** (காசு): Three soft, folded ten-rupee notes kept in Naveen's shirt pocket.
